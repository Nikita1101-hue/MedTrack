ABOUT US

MedTrack is a modern, unified healthcare management platform designed to make patient-doctor interactions simple, secure, and transparent. We combine intuitive appointment scheduling with encrypted digital diagnosis storage to ensure your care never stops.

Powered by high-availability cloud architecture with Amazon DynamoDB and AWS EC2, MedTrack keeps medical records confidential while ensuring they are accessible to patients and authorized doctors at a moment's notice.
